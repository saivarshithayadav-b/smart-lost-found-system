Help & Support

Find answers and get assistance with the Lost & Found system

How do I report a lost item?

Go to the lost item section, provide the required item details, and submit your report.

How do I report a found item?

Open the found item section and enter the details of the item you found before submitting the report.

How does item matching work?

The system compares information from lost and found item reports to identify possible matches.

What should I do if I find my item?

Check the relevant match information and follow the available recovery or contact process.

Still need help?

If you are facing a problem with the application, contact the system administrator or support team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I update my lost item report?

You can update the information associated with your report when the editing feature is available.

Can I report multiple items?

Yes, each lost or found item can be reported separately.

Why am I not seeing a match?

A match may not appear if the available lost and found item information does not correspond closely enough.
